The Best Pho in Winnipeg, by Neighbourhood

Winnipeg dining • 5 min read • May 11, 2026

The best pho in Winnipeg is spread across a few neighbourhoods rather than one strip. Pho Hoang has four branches (Sargent, Osborne, downtown Portage and Seasons of Tuxedo), T.H. Dang at 1425 Pembina Highway is the place for pho on Pembina, and the West End along Sargent and Ellice has several pho kitchens within a few blocks. Below is each one by area, with the days they close, so you can pick the bowl nearest to where you are.

Pho near me: which bowl is closest

  • Downtown: Pho Hoang Portage, 235 Portage Avenue, a short walk from Portage and Main.
  • Osborne Village and Crescentwood: Pho Hoang Osborne, 117 Osborne Street. Opens at 3 pm on weekdays.
  • West End: Pho Hoang Sargent (794 Sargent), Pho Binh Minh (819 Sargent), Pho Kim Tuong (856 Ellice) and Vi-Ann (1020 Notre Dame).
  • Fort Garry, along Pembina Highway: T.H. Dang, 1425 Pembina Highway.
  • Southwest, at Seasons of Tuxedo: Pho Hoang Seasons, 4-605 Sterling Lyon Parkway.

Pho Hoang: four branches, four sets of hours

Pho Hoang runs four branches, and they do not keep the same hours. Sargent and Seasons run 11 am to 9 pm Sunday to Thursday and until 10 pm on Friday and Saturday. The downtown room at 235 Portage keeps a shorter day, 11 am to 8 pm, which suits a lunch before an afternoon downtown. Osborne is the odd one: it does not open until 3 pm on weekdays (noon on weekends), then stays open until 11 pm (10 pm on Sunday), so it is the branch for a late bowl after a show at the Gas Station Arts Centre or a night in the Village.

Beyond pho, the menu runs to salad rolls and a sweet potato and shrimp crepe. The Sargent branch puts you in the middle of the West End strip if you want to compare bowls.

Pho on Pembina Highway: T.H. Dang

T.H. Dang Restaurant sits at 1425 Pembina Highway in Fort Garry, a family restaurant open since 2010. It is closed on Tuesdays; the other days run 11:30 am to 8 pm (noon on Sunday). Pho comes in about a dozen variations, and the menu goes well past it: bun bo hue, beef satay noodle soup, and the broken rice deluxe plate. It is the pick if you are heading south toward the University of Manitoba or coming back up Pembina from St. Adolphe.

One Pembina name to cross off: Pho Saigon, farther south at 2696 Pembina, has closed after the restaurant was sold.

The West End: Sargent and Ellice

If you want several options within a few blocks, go west of downtown. Pho Hoang's Sargent branch and Pho Binh Minh (101-819 Sargent) are on the same stretch of Sargent Avenue. Pho Binh Minh adds bubble tea to its pho and vermicelli, and has been known to close for a couple of weeks in summer for staff holidays, so check before a special trip.

Pho Kim Tuong is one street south at 856 Ellice Avenue. It is closed on Wednesdays and otherwise opens at 11 am. Our West End guide covers the murals and other stops nearby if you want to make an afternoon of it.

Vi-Ann Restaurant at 1020 Notre Dame Avenue is a little farther north; the menu includes a deluxe pho and a deluxe vermicelli bowl. Its posted hours differ from one listing to the next, so phone ahead.

Before you go: Small Vietnamese kitchens change hours without updating their listings, and several close one weekday. Check the restaurant's own site or call on the day.

Vegetarian? Pho Hoang offers tofu in its rolls and salads, but a vegetarian broth is a separate question from a tofu topping, so ask. Our Winnipeg vegan restaurant guide lists fully meat-free kitchens, and the wider Winnipeg restaurant guide covers everything else.

Stay nearby: Corydon Cute & Stylish is a three-bedroom house on Ruskin Row in Crescentwood, a short drive from the Osborne branch of Pho Hoang and from Pembina Highway. See our Osborne Village guide for what else is on that street.
