Private Dining in Winnipeg: Restaurants with Private Rooms

Group Dining • 7 min read • December 9, 2025

If you need a private dining room in Winnipeg for 10 to 60 people, start with Hy's Steakhouse at Portage and Main (four rooms, 16 to 60 seated), SMITH at The Forks (rooms for 8 to 50), or Chez-Nous in St. Boniface (three rooms, 24 to 48). For 100 or more, the restaurants with real banquet space are The Gates on Roblin (a ballroom for 350), Rae & Jerry's on Portage Avenue (groups up to 300), the King's Head Pub (a private space for 250) and The Saddlery on Market (330 in the building). Every one of them wants a phone call or an events form rather than an online booking once your group passes about ten.

This page is about restaurants: the food, staff and tables come with the room. If you need a hall, a museum or a taproom you rent and run yourself, see our list of event venues in Winnipeg for 30 to 2,500 guests.

Private dining rooms for 10 to 60

Hy's Steakhouse

Hy's is on the main floor of the Richardson Building at One Lombard Place. It has four closed rooms: the Kennedy Room seats 16, the Boardroom 20, the Clubroom 45 (65 for cocktails) and the Mezzanine 60. For a business dinner where people need to hear each other, the Boardroom is the one to ask for. More on the menu in our guide to Winnipeg steakhouses.

SMITH at the Inn at the Forks

SMITH, the restaurant inside the Inn at the Forks at 75 Forks Market Road, has a room for nearly every group size. The Harvest Table seats 8, The Cabin 12, the Library Table 20 and The Solstice 50 seated or 40 for a buffet. Take the whole restaurant and it holds up to 180. Being at The Forks also means the group can walk the river path before dinner.

529 Wellington

The steakhouse in a converted mansion on Wellington Crescent has private rooms for roughly 8 to 36 guests, most of them upstairs. There is no elevator, so if anyone in your party can't manage stairs, say so when you book. Reservations for private rooms go through the restaurant by phone.

Chez-Nous (formerly Chaise Café & Lounge)

The St. Boniface restaurant at 271 Provencher Boulevard now goes by Chez-Nous; if you knew it as Chaise, it is the same address. It serves groups a set family-style menu across three private rooms: the main-floor dining room for up to 48, the main-floor lounge for 32 and a second-floor room and lounge for 24. The garden-side patio takes 100 in summer. Each room carries its own minimum spend, and any booking of 10 or more has to be arranged by speaking to the staff; paying at least seven days ahead earns a 10 per cent credit.

Smaller rooms elsewhere

Fusion Grill at 550 Academy Road, a short drive west of Crescentwood, takes groups of up to 30. Bailey's Restaurant & Bar at 185 Lombard Avenue uses its upstairs dining room for private parties. Nicolino's on Pembina Highway has a back private room; call for its size.

Restaurants with banquet halls for 100 or more

The Gates on Roblin

Just west of the city in Headingley, on the banks of the Assiniboine, The Gates has private rooms at almost every size. Inside the restaurant: the Sunroom seats 72, the Bar and Lounge 50, the upstairs Study 24, and the Wine Cellar and Master Bedroom 14 each. The Grand Ballroom seats up to 350 (400 standing), and half of it takes 125. The restaurant is open Wednesday to Saturday for dinner and Sunday for brunch, so weekday events are worth asking about.

Rae & Jerry's Steak House

The long-running steakhouse at 1405 Portage Avenue has private rooms sized for groups of 30 to 35, 45 to 50, 85 to 90, 110 to 115 and 250 to 300. That last band is large enough for a wedding reception or a retirement dinner for a whole department. An 18 per cent gratuity is added to any party of 11 or more.

King's Head Pub and The Saddlery on Market

Two Exchange District options for a looser crowd. The King's Head at 120 King Street seats 340 and has a private space for up to 250 across its main and top floors, with catering from platters up to a full dinner menu; its curries share the menu with the pub standards. The Saddlery on Market at 114 Market Avenue holds up to 330 and has a private room for 80; it takes functions and larger group bookings in the evenings and on Sundays.

Clay Oven

Clay Oven's downtown restaurant at Blue Cross Park, 1 Portage Avenue East, handles large parties with a view over the ballpark. Its McPhillips Street location has private dining rooms and a banquet hall, and the Kenaston Boulevard location has a private event lounge. Its catering arm works for groups from 20 to 500 and more, at temples, halls and hotels across the city.

Birthday dinners and big family tables

For a birthday where the youngest guest is eight and the oldest is eighty, the Old Spaghetti Factory in the Johnston Terminal at The Forks is the easiest booking in the city: groups of nine or more use an enquiry form, and set group menus run $19.00, $22.50 and $27.25 a person before tax and gratuity. Ichiban at 189 Carlton Street, which has been cooking at the teppan grill for over 50 years, seats a party together around one grill, which is the whole point of going.

Gusto North, on the second floor of Hargrave St. Market at 242 Hargrave Street, takes private events of up to 85 seated or 100 standing indoors, plus 90 on its covered, heated patio. Its parent, Pizzeria Gusto at 404 Academy Road, is the better pick for a smaller table. In St. Boniface, Resto Gare, in a restored railway station at 630 Des Meurons Street, has hosted events from 4 to 100 guests and has a railcar lounge for a smaller group.

For lunch with children, Park Café in the Qualico Family Centre at Assiniboine Park seats 80 (120 standing), or 160 with the Tamarack Room added, and the duck pond is outside the window. Peasant Cookery in the Exchange District has no separate private room; call about taking over the dining room on a quiet night. Cibo Waterfront Café on Waterfront Drive books private work and social events, and Sabi on Taylor (the former Wasabi Sabi, at 1360 Taylor Avenue) is the sushi option for a group.

How large group reservations work here

  • Above about ten people, expect to phone or fill in an events form. Online booking usually stops short of that size.
  • Private rooms are priced by minimum spend, not a room fee: you commit to a food and drink total, and anything under it is billed anyway. Ask for the figure for your exact date.
  • Large parties carry an automatic gratuity, commonly 18 per cent. Rae & Jerry's applies it from 11 guests.
  • Set or family-style menus are standard for private rooms (groups at Chez-Nous get a set family-style menu). Send dietary needs with the final headcount.
  • Check stairs. Some rooms in older buildings, including at 529 Wellington, are on an upper floor without an elevator.

Before you go: Room sizes, minimum spends and set-menu prices change with the season. Confirm them with the restaurant's events contact for your exact date before you send invitations.

A room for the visitors in your group

When the dinner brings family or colleagues in from out of town, our three-bedroom house on Ruskin Row in Crescentwood is about a 10-minute walk from the restaurants on Corydon Avenue and a 9-minute drive from Portage and Main, where Hy's is. The Forks, for SMITH and the Old Spaghetti Factory, is about 10 minutes by car. For a smaller group dinner closer to home, our Corydon Avenue restaurant guide covers the neighbourhood.
