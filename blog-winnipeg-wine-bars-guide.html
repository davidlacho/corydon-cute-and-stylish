Wine Bars in Winnipeg, and the Wine Stores Worth a Trip

Winnipeg Guides • 4 min read • September 16, 2026

Winnipeg's wine bars are few and far apart: an Italian wine bar in St. Vital, a Spanish tapas bar and a coffee-and-wine room in the Exchange District, a late-evening wine list on Osborne Street, and a by-the-glass counter at The Forks. The wine shops worth a special trip are fewer still, because Manitoba allows only a handful of private wine stores. Below are the bars first, then the shops, with addresses and the hours each one keeps.

Wine bars in Winnipeg

Harth Mozza & Wine Bar, 980 St. Anne's Road (St. Vital)

Harth is an Italian wine bar in St. Vital, a part of the city most visitors never see. The kitchen makes its own pasta and cheese and serves them family-style: burrata with prosciutto and local honey ($26), a Neapolitan Margherita from the wood-fired oven ($24), ricotta tortellini in lemon butter ($33), and a $80-per-person "alla famiglia" spread if the whole table agrees. The wine list is chosen to match. Reservations open on Tock 30 days ahead and the largest table seats six, but part of the lounge and counter is held for walk-ins from 5 p.m., first come, first served.

Cordova Tapas & Wine, 93 Albert Street (Exchange District)

Cordova is a small Spanish tapas bar on Albert Street in the Exchange District, with an open kitchen behind the bar. The plates borrow from Spain and southwest France, and the list sticks to wine from France, Spain and Italy, with Spanish and Belgian beer on the side. It is open evenings only: 5 to 11 p.m. on Wednesday and Thursday and 5 p.m. to 1 a.m. on Friday and Saturday, which makes it one of the few places in the city to get a glass of Spanish red after a show. Pair it with our Exchange District guide.

The Oxbow, 557 Osborne Street (Lord Roberts)

The Oxbow sits on the southwest corner of Osborne Street and Morley Avenue, south of Osborne Village. The wine list runs through sparkling, white, rosé, orange and skin-contact, and red, with a deeper cellar list if you ask, plus cheese and small dishes from the kitchen. It is open 5 to 11 p.m. every night except Tuesday, and on Sunday the wine is half price. Of the bars on this page it is the closest to Corydon Avenue, a short drive or cab ride away.

Rosé Coffee & Wine, 474 Main Street (Exchange District)

Rosé is a coffee and wine bar with light plates, on Main Street at the edge of the Exchange District. It is the easy choice for one glass before a concert at the Centennial Concert Hall, a few blocks north.

The Common, The Forks Market

The Common is the bar in the middle of The Forks Market's food hall. It lists 22 wines across sparkling, rosé, white and red, changes the list often, and opens at 11 a.m. every day, so it is the one place on this page that pours wine at lunch. Buy a glass, then order food from any counter in the hall. More in our guide to The Forks.

Wine stores in Winnipeg

Most wine in Manitoba is sold through the province's Liquor Marts. The independent shops below are where to go for small producers, a sommelier's advice, or something you will not find on the Liquor Mart shelf.

Jones & Company Wine Merchants, 1616 St. Mary's Road (St. Vital)

Jones & Company opened in 1999 as Banville & Jones and took its current name in 2020, so older guides still use the first name. It has sommeliers on staff, WSET courses, and a text-a-sommelier line (204-400-0499) for advice before you come in. The store is open 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. Monday to Saturday and 11 a.m. to 6 p.m. on Sunday, and orders placed online can be picked up at the curb the same day. Harth is also in St. Vital, so the two make an easy evening together.

Ellement Wine + Spirits, The Forks Market

Ellement is a small shop inside The Forks Market, open 10 a.m. to 8 p.m. every day. Its buying leans to small producers and natural, low-intervention wine, including Burgundy and Beaujolais that are hard to find elsewhere in Manitoba, and the staff will talk you through a bottle before you buy it. It is the easiest stop if you want a bottle to take back to the house after a walk along the rivers.

Kenaston Wine Market, 1855A Grant Avenue

Kenaston Wine Market is a private wine store on Grant Avenue, near Kenaston Boulevard in the southwest. It also delivers through the usual food apps, which helps on a cold night when you would rather not go out.

Planning a wine evening

The bars do not cluster. Harth and Jones & Company are in St. Vital, Cordova and Rosé are in the Exchange District, The Oxbow is on Osborne, and The Common is at The Forks, so treat this as separate outings rather than a wine crawl on foot. Two pairings work: a bottle at Jones & Company and then dinner at Harth, or a glass at The Common in the afternoon and tapas at Cordova once it opens at 5. Cordova is closed Sunday to Tuesday and The Oxbow on Tuesday, so plan around Tuesday, when neither is open.

Before you go: Wine bar hours shift with the seasons and around holidays. Check each bar's own site before you head out, and book Harth ahead if you need a table rather than a counter seat.
