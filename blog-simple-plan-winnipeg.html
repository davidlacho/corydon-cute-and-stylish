Simple Plan in Winnipeg: February 2, 2027 at Canada Life Centre

Winnipeg Guides • 5 min read • November 28, 2026

Simple Plan play Canada Life Centre, 300 Portage Avenue, on Tuesday, February 2, 2027, and the arena's own listing gives one time: 7 p.m. We have sent enough guests downtown for arena nights to know which parts of an evening like this catch people out, and with this one the trouble starts with the day of the week. A Tuesday show in February is a different animal from a Saturday show in June.

Three bands, a 7 p.m. start, and a weeknight

The arena names only Simple Plan and gives no doors time. The tour's own listing for the Bigger Than You Think! Canada run puts Yellowcard and Bowling For Soup on the bill with them, which is the fact that should shape your arrival. Three bands and a 7 p.m. start do not leave room for a leisurely first hour; on a bill like this the opener goes on close to the stated time rather than an hour after it. If you want to see all three, we would be through screening by 6.40 at the latest.

It will be dark and it will be cold. Sunset in Winnipeg on February 2 is 5.24 p.m., and the day runs to nine hours and twenty-five minutes of light, so the city has been dark for an hour and a half before the first chord. The useful detail for a February night is Gate 6, which opens off the Cityplace Skywalk: you can reach the arena from the parkade and the mall side without going outside at all. Gate 1 at Portage and Donald and Gate 3 at Graham and Hargrave both put you on the sidewalk first.

The bag rule, and the one thing a Tuesday makes easier

The venue limit is 4 by 6 by 1.5 inches. That is a phone and a card holder. Not a tote, not a small backpack, and there is no bag check anywhere on site, so a bag turned away at the X-ray has to go back to the car or the room before you come in again. Medical bags and childcare or diaper bags are exempt. Individual tours can set a stricter rule of their own, and when they do the arena emails ticket holders in advance, which is worth reading rather than deleting.

Here is where the weeknight helps. On a weekday in hockey season the box office at Gate 1, street level at the corner of Portage Avenue and Donald Street, opens at 11 a.m. and stays open until event time, instead of the two-hours-before rule that applies on a weekend. If you need a will-call pickup, a seat changed or a question answered by a person, a Tuesday gives you most of the day to do it. The number is 204-987-7825. Tickets themselves went on sale on Friday, October 9 at 10 a.m. and the arena links to Ticketmaster from its event page, so this date has had months on the market.

Where we would eat first

Hargrave St. Market, at 242 Hargrave Street in True North Square, is a block from the arena and the answer we give most often, because a group that cannot agree on dinner can order from several kitchens and still sit together. Our guide to Hargrave St. Market covers who is cooking in there now. A 5.30 table is early for a Tuesday, which is exactly why it works: downtown kitchens are quieter on a weeknight than they are before a weekend show, and you are not racing a hockey crowd for a table.

By bus, Portage Avenue is the arena's own street, and the BLUE line runs the Southwest Transitway and then along Portage, which makes it the simple route from Crescentwood and Corydon with no transfer. Fares and trip planning are on winnipegtransit.com.

Before you go: Start times shift and tours tighten the bag rule on their own. Confirm the time, the open gates and the current bag limit at canadalifecentre.ca in the week before February 2.

Where to stay for the February 2 show

Our three-bedroom house in Crescentwood is a 10 minute drive from Canada Life Centre, 3.9 km by road, and the BLUE line connects the two directly. On a Tuesday that distance earns its keep twice: once getting in without hunting for a parkade spot, and once at the end, when a bill with three bands on it finishes late and the next morning is a working one.
