Spring Break in Winnipeg 2027: March 29 to April 2

Winnipeg Guides • 5 min read • October 9, 2026

Manitoba's provincial school calendar puts spring break at Monday, March 29 through Friday, April 2, 2027. Good Friday falls the Friday before, March 26, so most families effectively get a long weekend tacked onto the front of the week rather than a clean five-day block. Individual divisions can shift a day here or there, so a family travelling in from outside the province should still check their own school's calendar; the provincial dates are the pattern everyone else builds around.

Three different things get called "spring break programming" in Winnipeg, and they are not aimed at the same visitor. Here is how they actually compare.

Free and built for kids: the library and the community centre

The city's own spring break programming runs through two systems that rarely get mentioned in the same sentence. Winnipeg Public Library runs free, registration-based sessions at branches across the city during the break; past years have covered writing workshops, robotics, and short performances, with the full lineup posted at winnipeg.ca/library closer to the date and sign-up handled at any branch. Separately, the city's recreation services have run free drop-in play for ages six to twelve at community centres during the same week; one recent year had River Osborne Community Centre open noon to mid-afternoon on four of the five days. Indoor pools have added reduced-fee or free swim times on top of that. None of this costs anything beyond a bus fare, and none of it needs to be booked more than a day or two out. The trade-off is that it is scattered by branch and by centre, so you are planning around what is near you rather than around one single draw.

Ticketed and mostly for adults: what the Centennial Concert Hall has on

It is tempting to assume the Royal Winnipeg Ballet and Royal MTC have something timed to the break. They mostly do not. RWB's Swan Lake runs March 4 to 7, 2027 at the Centennial Concert Hall, which wraps up three full weeks before spring break starts; the company's next Mainstage program, The Mix, a triple bill including Balanchine's Rubies, opens April 29 and runs to May 2, nearly a month after the break ends, with a $15 Community Matinee on April 30 for groups of ten or more. Royal MTC's Waiting for Godot does run through the break itself, March 24 to April 17, but it is Beckett, not family theatre, so it reads better as a parents' night out once the kids are asleep at the Airbnb than as something to bring them to. If a specific evening at either venue matters to your trip, book it on its own date rather than assuming it lines up with the school calendar.

A bigger day out: the attractions that are always open

For a visitor who wants one or two bigger outings rather than a week of smaller free ones, our Classic 107 spring break roundup covers the museums, splash pads, and community-mall play areas that run on their own hours year-round rather than on a break-week schedule. That list is the better starting point if you have one full day to fill and a car; this page is about the programming that only exists because the schools are closed.

Whichever way you split the week, late March in Winnipeg is still winter's tail end. Normal daytime highs sit around 8 degrees Celsius by early April, nights can still drop below freezing, and a last snowfall is not unusual even once the break starts. Layers and boots that can handle slush beat a spring jacket packed on faith.

Where to stay for spring break

Our three-bedroom home in Crescentwood, a few blocks south of Corydon Avenue, puts you roughly ten minutes by car from downtown and the Centennial Concert Hall, and a similar distance from most of the community centres running drop-in programs across the city's south end. A kitchen and a second bedroom matter more on a week built around naps, snow boots drying by the door, and a library sign-up sheet than they do on a single-night stay. Spring break is a known pressure point on availability here, so it is worth booking before the library posts its own schedule rather than after. Our Airbnb listing shows what is open that week.

Before you go: The library's 2027 branch schedule and the city's 2027 recreation and pool hours are posted at winnipeg.ca/library and winnipeg.ca closer to the break. Confirm your specific community centre's hours there before you plan a day around one.
