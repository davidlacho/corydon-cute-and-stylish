Event Venues in Winnipeg: Spaces for 30 to 2,500 Guests

Event Planning • 8 min read • December 9, 2025

The short answer: Winnipeg's largest bookable banquet room is Exhibition Place at Red River Exhibition Park, which seats up to 2,500 for dinner, and the smallest rooms worth renting hold about 30, such as the rooftop at Little Brown Jug Brewing. Between those sit museum halls, a concert-hall lobby, golf-club ballrooms and brewery taprooms. The capacities below are the venues' own published figures, sorted by the size of group they suit, with the catering and booking rules that catch planners out.

This page covers venues you rent. If you would rather book a private dining room or take over a restaurant for the night, our guide to Winnipeg restaurants with private rooms for large groups covers that side.

Halls for 500 to 2,500 guests

Red River Exhibition Park

Exhibition Place, a 35,750-square-foot hall on the western edge of the city at 3977 Portage Avenue, seats up to 2,500 people for a sit-down dinner. It is the largest seated figure on this list. The catch is food and drink: the Exhibition Association keeps the rights to all food and beverage sales unless you negotiate otherwise, and it asks for proof of liability insurance 15 days before the event.

Centennial Concert Hall

The lobby levels of the concert hall at 555 Main Street take standing receptions of up to 1,500 and seated meals of up to 600. For something smaller, the Piano Nobile holds 380 standing, and the Red Room takes 300 standing or 88 seated. You can bring an outside caterer, but the bar is run in-house.

The Metropolitan Entertainment Centre

The Met, the former Metropolitan Theatre at 281 Donald Street, now run by Canad Inns, holds up to 1,000 standing across the whole building. Its Grand Hall seats 350 at round tables (300 with a dance floor) or 450 for a reception. All food is prepared in-house. The rooftop patio, open in summer only, adds room for 140 seated or 180 standing.

Canadian Museum for Human Rights

Bonnie & John Buhler Hall, in the museum at The Forks, takes 750 for a reception or 350 for dinner. The museum also rents smaller rooms: the Stuart Clark Garden of Contemplation (80 for dinner, 150 for a reception), Bistro Boréal (60 and 90) and the Carolyn Sifton Foundation Terrace (50 and 100).

Manitoba Museum

Alloway Hall seats 500 for dinner or holds 600 standing; book the galleries as well and the figure rises to 680. Guests can drink among the exhibits: the Nonsuch Gallery, built around the full-size replica ketch, takes 150 standing or 32 at a table. Festival Hall (100 seated, 225 standing) suits a mid-sized dinner.

Canada Life Centre and the RBC Convention Centre

Both are downtown and both are the obvious names for a large corporate event, but neither publishes a room capacity. Canada Life Centre rents the event floor and the Portage Avenue atrium for dinners, trade shows and private parties on non-event days only, and it will not rent the ice. The RBC Convention Centre at 375 York Avenue has a 24,000-square-foot ballroom and 131,000 square feet of connected hall space on its third floor. For either, send your headcount and layout and ask for a floor plan.

Corporate event venues for 100 to 650

Conferences and company dinners tend to land in this band, and the universities are the cheapest way in. The University of Manitoba's Manitoba Room, in the University Centre on the Fort Garry campus, handles banquets of 200 to 650; Pembina Hall's dining room (175 to 350) is available only from May to August. Catering there goes through the campus caterer, with a per-person minimum. Canadian Mennonite University on Shaftesbury Boulevard has a Great Hall that seats 120 at round tables and the Laudamus Auditorium for up to 250 in presentation seating.

For a sports crowd, the Pinnacle Club on the east side of Princess Auto Stadium takes formal banquets of up to 350 and cocktail receptions of up to 400. Assiniboia Downs, out on Portage Avenue near the Perimeter, has the Finish Line Banquet Room for 30 to 300 on its lower level; check the racing calendar before you pick a date. In St. Boniface, the Centre culturel franco-manitobain at 340 Provencher Boulevard lists 554 seats in the Jean-Paul Aubry Room and 285 in its amphitheatre.

Office party and holiday party venues

Assiniboine Park Conservancy is the one operator that names holiday parties outright, and it has rooms at most sizes. The Leaf's banquet hall seats 260 or holds 350 standing. At the zoo, Gateway to the Arctic takes 250 for a reception (88 seated), and the Tundra Grill seats 100. The Pavilion Atrium suits a smaller team: 96 seated or 120 standing.

Kitchen Sync, a rental kitchen and event room at 370 Donald Street, seats up to 60 and is licensed for 90 people in total, counting staff and vendors. It sells six-hour packages from $500 to $1,000 on weekdays and $750 to $1,500 on weekends and holidays, with a $750 alcohol minimum. Breweries are the other easy answer for a December party. Torque Brewing, at 830 King Edward Street, books its taproom for up to 100, with a three-hour minimum. La Brasserie Nonsuch in the Exchange District takes 84 seated or 100 standing for a full buyout, with a minimum spend and an 18 per cent gratuity. Our guide to Winnipeg breweries has more on each taproom.

December dates: If your party has to be on a Friday or Saturday in early December, book the room as soon as you have a headcount. Moving it to a Thursday, or to January, widens the choice of rooms considerably.

Small party venues for 30 to 80

For a birthday, a shower or a team dinner of a few dozen, a full hall feels empty. These rooms are built for that size. Little Brown Jug Brewing at 336 William Avenue rents its rooftop level for up to 30 and asks for at least three weeks' notice for any private booking. Capital K Distillery, in an industrial unit on Dublin Avenue, hosts up to 38 guests and runs private cocktail classes for groups of up to 14.

Fort Gibraltar, the reconstructed fur-trade post in Whittier Park, rents the Partner's Room for 48 seated or 60 standing and its Great Hall for 140 seated or 183 standing. The Pavilion Galleries in Assiniboine Park take 48 seated or 75 standing. Closest to Crescentwood, the Corydon Community Centre rents a multi-purpose room for 75 standing and a social hall for up to 340, at a flat $1,695 for a social.

East of the city near Oakbank, Pineridge Hollow rents its Village Studio for up to 30, a better fit for a small corporate retreat than a party.

Venues for a celebration of life

A memorial reception needs a room on short notice, a kitchen and staff who have done it before. Assiniboine Park lists celebrations of life, memorials and funeral receptions among the events it hosts, so the rooms above (Park Café and the Tamarack Room together seat 160) are available for them. Niakwa Country Club, at 620 Niakwa Road, sells a Celebration of Life package; its Hazelton Room holds up to 30, and the Grand Ballroom seats 200 or takes 300 for a reception, with all catering and bar through the club.

For an all-inclusive option, Ethical Death Care runs a celebration-of-life package at 603 Wellington Crescent for $6,695 plus GST, with catering for 50 guests and extra guests charged per head. St. Boniface Golf Club at 100 Youville Street seats 220 and sells all-inclusive packages with free parking, which matters when older relatives are driving.

Out of town: heritage sites and nature centres

Lower Fort Garry National Historic Site, north of the city on Highway 9 near Selkirk, seats 115 for a banquet in the visitor centre's multi-purpose room; Parks Canada prices each booking individually. FortWhyte Alive in southwest Winnipeg takes 140 for cocktails in its Alloway Reception Centre (40 to 110 for a plated dinner) and has a second room that seats up to 150. Oak Hammock Marsh, north of the city, rents its conference centre for 120 to 150 for $2,000 a day or evening. The Royal Aviation Museum at 2088 Wellington Avenue, beside the airport, holds 500 in the museum and up to 165 in its Riley Meeting Room.

Golf clubs fill the gap between a hall and a restaurant. Besides Niakwa and St. Boniface, Transcona Country Club on Dugald Road has a Renaissance Ballroom for up to 300.

Before you go: Room capacities change with the layout (rounds, theatre seating, a dance floor), and venues revise their rate cards each year. Confirm the figure for your layout, and the catering rules, with the venue's events office before you sign.

Staying nearby for an event

Our three-bedroom house on Ruskin Row in Crescentwood is a 7-minute drive from the RBC Convention Centre and about 10 minutes from Canada Life Centre, the Centennial Concert Hall and The Forks. If you are deciding between a venue and a restaurant, a restaurant's private room is usually simpler for under 40 people because the food, staff and tables come with it; above that, a venue gives you more control.
