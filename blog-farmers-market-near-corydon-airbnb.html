Seasonal produce and the nearest farmers' market to the Corydon Airbnb

Winnipeg Guides • 4 min read • August 4, 2026

You don't need a car or a big detour to catch a weekly farmers' market while staying near Corydon Avenue. The closest option runs a few blocks over in River Heights, and it lands right in the middle of Manitoba's peak growing season.

The closest option: River Heights Farmers' Market

The River Heights Farmers' Market sets up in the parking lot at 1370 Grosvenor Ave (a few blocks off Corydon Avenue) every Friday from 12pm to 5pm, running from July 3 through September 25, 2026. It's organized by the Corydon Community Centre and features locally grown produce, fruit, flowers, baked goods, and crafts from area vendors. Because it runs Fridays through late September, it overlaps with most guest stays during the summer season, worth checking the Community Centre's page above for that week's vendor list before you go.

What's in season right now

Early August is a strong stretch for Manitoba produce: sweet corn, field tomatoes, peppers, beans, and cucumbers are all at or near their peak, alongside local berries. What individual vendors bring changes week to week, so treat this as a general guide rather than a guarantee of what will be on a specific table.

Want a bigger market trip?

If you have a car or more time to spend, our Winnipeg farmers' markets guide covers the larger St. Norbert and Downtown BIZ markets, both of which run bigger vendor lists but sit further from Corydon.

From Corydon: River Heights Farmers' Market is the nearest weekly option to the Airbnb, bring a reusable bag and some cash, since not every small vendor takes cards.
