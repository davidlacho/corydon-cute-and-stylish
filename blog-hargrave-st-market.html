Hargrave St. Market: Menu, Vendors and Hours

Food & Dining • 4 min read • December 5, 2025

Hargrave St. Market is the food hall on the first and second levels of True North Square, 242 Hargrave Street, across from Canada Life Centre. The hall is open Monday to Friday 7:30 a.m. to 10 p.m. and Saturday 9 a.m. to 11 p.m., and it is closed on Sunday; the Lake of the Woods taproom opens on a Sunday only when there is an event at Canada Life Centre. Seven vendors share the hall: Gusto North, Yard Burger, The Good Fight Taco, Saburo Kitchen, Empty Cup, Rose Bar and Lake of the Woods Brewing Company. Each counter keeps its own hours inside the market's, and several close well before the hall does, so the vendor list below gives each one's hours where it publishes them.

Hargrave St. Market hours

  • Monday to Friday: 7:30 a.m. to 10 p.m.
  • Saturday: 9 a.m. to 11 p.m.
  • Sunday: closed (Lake of the Woods opens two hours before a Canada Life Centre event)

Those are the building's hours. The bars and Gusto North carry the evening. Saburo Kitchen closes at 7:30 p.m. on weekdays, so on a Jets night eat there early or pick another counter.

The vendors, their menus and their hours

Gusto North

Italian food from the team behind Pizzeria Gusto on Academy Road, on the second floor, and the only sit-down, full-service restaurant in the market. The menu runs from pizza (Margherita $23, Calabrese $24) to handmade pasta (Limone Amalfi $25, Carbonara $34) and mains like grilled pork and salmon. On hockey nights a cheese or pepperoni pizza is $12. It is open 11:30 a.m. to 10 p.m. Monday to Thursday, 11:30 a.m. to 11 p.m. on Friday, and 3 to 11 p.m. on Saturday; book through OpenTable on the Gusto North site.

Saburo Kitchen

Ramen and donburi rice bowls from the chefs behind Yujiro and GaiJin Izakaya, in unit 240 on the second floor. Extras let you build the bowl up: more pork belly, a marinated egg, an extra portion of noodles or broth, or a dan dan pork chop. The Saburo Kitchen counter is open 11 a.m. to 7:30 p.m. Monday to Thursday, 11 a.m. to 8 p.m. on Friday, and noon to 8 p.m. on Saturday.

Yard Burger

Beef, chicken and veggie burgers with house sauces, fresh-cut fries and cold drinks. The quick choice before a game or a concert, and it delivers through DoorDash.

The Good Fight Taco

Tacos, bowls and sides from the people behind Merchant Kitchen, which mixes Asian and Latin American cooking. Expect bright, saucy fillings rather than a traditional taqueria menu.

Empty Cup

A Winnipeg coffee company that started in 2020 and now runs several cafés; here it pours coffee and nitro cold brew. Our Winnipeg coffee guide covers the other independents.

Rose Bar

The bar in the middle of the second floor, with cocktails, wine and local and imported beer on tap. You can bring food from any of the counters to the bar. Rose Bar opens at 3 p.m. Monday to Saturday and stays open until 10 p.m. Monday to Thursday and 11 p.m. on Friday and Saturday.

Lake of the Woods Brewing Company

The cottage-country brewery from Kenora brews on the market's second level and runs its taproom and retail store on the ground floor, with a door on Carlton Street. The taproom bakes its own wood-fired pizza, and you can carry food down from the upstairs counters. It opens at noon Monday to Friday (until 9 p.m. Monday to Wednesday, 11 p.m. Thursday and Friday) and 11 a.m. to 11 p.m. on Saturday. More in our Winnipeg breweries guide.

Is there a Hargrave St. Market menu?

There is no single menu. Each vendor keeps its own, and the market's website links to them along with online ordering and catering. Order at the counter you want, then find a seat in the shared seating; the exception is Gusto North, where you are seated and served. A mixed group can order from three counters and still eat at one table, which makes the hall an easy answer for group dining downtown when nobody agrees on a cuisine.

True North Square restaurants and the food court

If you searched for the True North Square food court or the restaurants at True North Square, this is it. The square is the cluster of towers and the plaza south of Graham Avenue between Hargrave and Carlton streets, and Hargrave St. Market, open since December 2019, is its food hall. The plaza outside has an outdoor stage in summer, a skating rink in winter, and the Dale Hawerchuk statue unveiled in 2022.

Before you go: Vendors here change more often than in a standalone restaurant, and each sets its own hours. Check the current list and hours at hargravestmarket.com before you plan a meal around one counter.

Reaching the market from the street, the skywalk or the Graham Avenue buses

You walk in from street level, with escalators and an elevator between the two floors. In winter you do not need to go outside at all: the market is tied into downtown's skywalk network. Buses stop on the Graham Avenue transit mall, right outside; our transit basics guide covers fares and routes from the Corydon side of town.

Expect the hall at its busiest in the hour or two before puck drop on Jets nights, with fans eating before they cross to Canada Life Centre; come early or once the game has started. Our Jets game-day guide has the rest of the evening.

Staying in Crescentwood: Corydon Cute & Stylish, our three-bedroom house on Ruskin Row, is about a 10-minute drive from Canada Life Centre, across the street from the market. Pair a market lunch with the Exchange District, the Winnipeg Art Gallery or The Forks.
