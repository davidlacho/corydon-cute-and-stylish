Hargrave St. Market: Hours, Vendors and the True North Square Food Hall

Food & Dining • 5 min read • December 5, 2025

Hargrave St. Market is the food hall on the first and second levels of True North Square, 242 Hargrave Street, across from Canada Life Centre. It is open Monday to Friday 7:30 am to 10 pm and Saturday 9 am to 11 pm, and closed on Sunday, except for the Lake of the Woods Brewing taproom, which opens noon to 8 pm. Seven vendors share the hall: Gusto North, Yard Burger, The Good Fight Taco, Saburo Kitchen, Empty Cup, Rose Bar and Lake of the Woods Brewing Company. Each counter sets its own hours inside the market's, so a coffee bar can be dark at 9 pm while the bar is still pouring.

True North Square restaurants and the food court

If you searched for the True North Square food court or the restaurants at True North Square, this is it. The square is the cluster of towers and the two-acre plaza south of Graham Avenue between Hargrave and Carlton streets, and Hargrave St. Market, open since December 2019, is its food hall. The counters are run by local and regional businesses, and one of them, Gusto North, is a full-service restaurant with table service. The plaza outside has an outdoor stage in summer, a skating rink in winter, and the Dale Hawerchuk statue unveiled in 2022.

The seven vendors and what each one serves

Gusto North. The first offshoot of Winnipeg's Pizzeria Gusto, serving Italian food family-style. It is the only sit-down, full-service restaurant in the market, so it is the one to choose if you want a server and a proper table.

Yard Burger. Beef, chicken and veggie burgers with fresh-cut fries. A straightforward choice before a game or a concert.

The Good Fight Taco. Tacos, bowls and sides that borrow from Asian and Latin American cooking at once, which is the point of the place.

Saburo Kitchen. Ramen and donburi rice bowls from the chefs behind Yujiro and GaiJin Izakaya, two of the city's better-known Japanese kitchens.

Empty Cup. A Winnipeg coffee company that started in 2020 and now runs several cafés around the city; here it pours coffee and nitro cold brew. Fools & Horses no longer has a counter here. Our Winnipeg coffee guide covers the other independents.

Rose Bar. The bar in the middle of the hall, with cocktails, wine, and local and imported beer on tap.

Lake of the Woods Brewing Company. The cottage-country brewery from Kenora brews on the market's second level and runs its tasting room and retail store on the first floor. It is the one part of the market open on Sunday. More on it in our Winnipeg breweries guide.

Menus and ordering

There is no single Hargrave St. Market menu. Each vendor keeps its own, and the market's website links to them along with online ordering and catering. Order at the counter you want, then find a seat in the shared seating; the exception is Gusto North, where you are seated and served. A mixed group can order from three counters and still eat at one table, which makes the hall an easy answer for group dining downtown when nobody agrees on a cuisine.

Before you go: Vendors here change more often than in a standalone restaurant, and each sets its own hours. Check the current list and hours at hargravestmarket.com before you plan a meal around one counter.

Reaching the market from the street, the skywalk or the Graham Avenue buses

You walk in from street level, with escalators and an elevator between the two floors. In winter you do not need to go outside at all: the market is tied into downtown's skywalk network. Buses stop on the Graham Avenue transit mall, right outside; our transit basics guide covers fares and routes from the Corydon side of town.

Expect the hall at its busiest in the hour or two before puck drop on Jets nights, with fans eating before they cross to Canada Life Centre; come early or once the game has started. Our Jets game-day guide has the rest of the evening.

Staying in Crescentwood: Corydon Cute & Stylish, our three-bedroom house on Ruskin Row, is about a 10-minute drive from Canada Life Centre, across the street from the market. Pair a market lunch with the Exchange District, the Winnipeg Art Gallery or The Forks.
