Beating the summer heat near the Corydon Airbnb

☀️ Winnipeg Guides • ⏱️ 4 min read • 📅 August 8, 2026

Winnipeg summers run hot, and on the warmest days you don't need to go far from the Corydon/Crescentwood Airbnb to cool off. A few cold drinks, one air-conditioned stop, and a shaded park cover most of a hot afternoon.

Cold drinks without leaving Corydon Avenue

Thom Bargen Coffee Roasters (743 Corydon Ave) is a reliable stop for iced coffee and cold brew. A couple of doors down, The Mighty Kiwi Juice Bar & Eatery (709 Corydon Ave) does smoothies and fresh juice if you want something fruitier than coffee. Sugar + Salt Bakeshoppe (897 Corydon Ave) is worth a stop too if you'd rather grab something sweet to go and keep walking in the shade of the storefronts along the strip.

An air-conditioned afternoon

If the heat gets to be too much, The Leaf at Assiniboine Park is the best indoor option in the area. Its tropical and Mediterranean biomes are climate-controlled year-round, and as of this summer the site is open daily from 9am to 9pm, with paid admission to the biomes (the surrounding gardens are free to walk). Hours and ticket prices can shift by season, so check the Assiniboine Park Conservancy's site before you head over. See our Assiniboine Park guide for more on getting there and what else is on-site.

Shade and a slower pace nearby

Assiniboine Park's mature tree canopy and riverbank paths make for a much cooler walk than the open sidewalks downtown. Closer to the Airbnb, Enderton Park (Peanut Park) has enough shade trees around its playground and benches to make a good spot to sit with a cold drink from Corydon Avenue rather than carrying it home. See our Peanut Park guide for more.

From Corydon: Everything above is either on Corydon Avenue itself or a short drive/rideshare from the Airbnb, so a hot day doesn't have to mean a long outing to stay comfortable.
