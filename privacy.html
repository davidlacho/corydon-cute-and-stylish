Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 September 2026

This page explains what happens to information when you visit staywinnipeg.ca. The site is a static marketing site for a single short-term rental in Winnipeg, Manitoba. It has no accounts, no login, no shopping cart, and no contact form, so there is nothing here for you to submit and nothing for us to store about you directly.

What this site does not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, or payment details anywhere on this site. Booking and payment happen entirely on Airbnb, under Airbnb's own terms and privacy policy, not ours.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-70X1LQFQ5R) to understand which pages people read and roughly where visitors come from. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and receives your IP address, which Google truncates before storing. We use it in aggregate only: we look at page counts and traffic sources, never at individual visitors, and we do not attempt to identify anyone from it.

You can opt out at any time by installing the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, by blocking cookies for this site, or by using your browser's tracking protection. Nothing on the site stops working if you do.

Other services that load with our pages

A handful of third parties serve files or embedded content on this site. When your browser requests those files, it necessarily reveals your IP address and browser details to the provider, in the same way as visiting any website. Each one has its own privacy policy:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com), which serves the two typefaces used across the site.
  • unpkg (unpkg.com), which serves the JavaScript libraries the pages are built with, including React and the Leaflet library behind the location map.
  • Unsplash (images.unsplash.com), which serves some illustrative photographs in blog posts. Photographs of the home itself are hosted by us.
  • Wikimedia Commons (upload.wikimedia.org), which serves an archival photograph used in one blog post.
  • OpenStreetMap, which supplies the map tiles on the location map.
  • Viator, which supplies the booking widget on the Discounted Tours and Experiences page. If you book a tour through that widget, Viator handles the transaction and your data under its own policy.

Affiliate links

The tours and experiences we list are Viator affiliate links. If you book through them we may earn a commission at no additional cost to you. We do not receive your booking details.

Cookies

The only cookies this site sets are the Google Analytics cookies described above. We set no advertising cookies and run no advertising or retargeting pixels. Embedded third-party content, such as the Viator widget, may set its own cookies when it loads.

Your choices and your rights

Because we hold no personal information about site visitors, there is nothing for us to look up, correct, export, or delete on request. If you want data held by Google, Viator, or Airbnb about you, those requests need to go to them directly. If you have booked a stay with us, information about that booking lives in Airbnb's systems and is governed by Airbnb's privacy policy.

Children

This site is aimed at adults planning travel and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this page

If we add a service that changes any of the above, we will update this page and change the date at the top of it.
