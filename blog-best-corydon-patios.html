Best patios on and near Corydon this season

📍 Winnipeg Guides • ⏱️ 6 min read • 📅 July 24, 2026

Corydon Avenue turns into one long patio strip once the weather holds, and most of it is a short walk from the Airbnb. Here's where to sit outside this season, grouped by the kind of afternoon or evening you're after.

Rooftop and elevated seating

The Roost (651 Corydon Ave) has a rooftop patio with cocktails and shareable plates, a good pick for an evening with a view over the strip rather than street level. Further down at Confusion Corner Bar and Grill (500 Corydon Ave), the rooftop patio is semi-covered and heated, so it stretches further into shoulder-season evenings than an open-air spot would.

Courtyard and streetside classics

Saffron's Restaurant (681 Corydon Ave) has a quieter, greenery-filled patio that suits a relaxed lunch away from the busiest sidewalk tables. Bar Italia (737 Corydon Ave) is the opposite mode: its streetside patio runs along the busiest block of Corydon and is built for people-watching. Nearby, Saperavi Georgian Cuisine (709 Corydon Ave) shares a patio with the Danish spot next door, worth knowing if you want two different menus without changing tables.

Planning around patio season

Manitoba's patio season generally runs from late spring through early fall, weather permitting, and coverage varies a lot between fully open sidewalk seating and heated rooftop setups like Confusion Corner's. Because hours and patio availability can shift with weather and staffing, it's worth checking a restaurant's own site or calling ahead before you walk over, especially on a weekday or outside peak summer.

From Corydon: Every patio above is within about a 10-minute walk of the Airbnb, so it's easy to patio-hop between a rooftop spot and a streetside table in the same evening without needing transit or a car.
