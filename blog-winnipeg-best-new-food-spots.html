New Restaurants in Winnipeg: The 2026 Openings and the Best of 2025

Winnipeg Dining • 4 min read • December 11, 2025

The new restaurants in Winnipeg worth planning a trip around are clustered in a few places: Osborne Village, Corydon and Grant Avenues, The Forks Market and the Exchange District. This list covers what opened in 2025 and 2026 and is still open, with a street address for each, starting with the four 2026 openings and then the class of 2025 by neighbourhood. Snack Häus, a 2025 opening in Fort Rouge, was named the third-best new restaurant in Canada by Air Canada in 2026, the highest any Winnipeg room has placed.

New restaurants in Winnipeg in 2026

Peacock Kitchen & Drinks, 1822 Grant Avenue (River Heights)

Chef Ed Lam and Esther Lo opened Peacock on February 6, 2026, and by May it had placed 68th on Canada's 100 Best. The cooking is pan-Asian and detailed: lamb soup dumplings shaped like cappelletti, lobster risotto with char siu on the tail. Book ahead for a weekend.

Sottobraccio, 160 Osborne Street (Osborne Village)

A modern Italian room of about 70 seats that opened in June 2026 and run by chefs and co-owners Brent Genyk and Brady Palmer. The menu is built for sharing: radiatori, ricotta tortellini and cioppino.

House of Ma Kitchen + Bar, 110-127 Bannatyne Avenue (Exchange District)

House of Ma opened in late July 2026 in the East Exchange. It is pan-Asian with a Filipino streak, under executive chef Dom Merano; the sizzling pork-belly sisig is the dish to order.

Dos Pampas Skyline, 83 Garry Street (Downtown)

The revolving restaurant on the 30th floor of Fort Garry Place reopened as Dos Pampas Skyline on October 9, 2026. The food is Argentine: dry-aged beef and empanadas with chimichurri. You pay for the view, and in late November the sun is down by about 4:30, so book an early table if you want to see the city in daylight.

The best new restaurants of 2025, by neighbourhood

Osborne Village: BabyBaby and Shirley's

The two openings that put Winnipeg on the national lists in 2025 sit side by side. Shirley's, at 135 Osborne Street, opened on March 14, 2025: chef Renée Girard, 35 seats, Italian-leaning pastas and a small gourmet grocery. The carrot cavatelli and the fries with smoked mussel aioli are the usual first orders. Next door at 137 Osborne Street, BabyBaby opened the same month, with shared plates such as walleye beignets and whole grilled sea bass. Both were finalists for Air Canada's Best New Restaurants in 2025. Our Osborne Village guide covers the rest of the street.

Fort Rouge: Snack Häus

Snack Häus, at 398 Daly Street North inside Low Life Barrel House, a few blocks down Pembina from Confusion Corner, opened in October 2025. Chef Dustin Pajak cooks Spanish-leaning small plates (broccoli empanada with gouda mornay, tuna tostada, beer-can chicken) and keeps a separate vegan menu. In 2026 it came third on Air Canada's Best New Restaurants list and got a special mention from Canada's 100 Best.

Corydon and Grant Avenue: MLYN, Naan Culture and The Green Falafel

MLYN, at Unit 1, 709 Corydon Avenue (below Saperavi), opened in summer 2025 and cooks Ukrainian dishes: deruny, varenyky, kruchenyky and shuba. Farther west, Naan Culture opened in late May 2025 at 20-1700 Corydon Avenue, where the naan comes stuffed with chicken korma, beef birria or shahi paneer. On Grant, The Green Falafel at Unit B, 1833 Grant Avenue opened in July 2025 and is entirely vegetarian, with green Israeli-style falafel, house-made pita and shakshuka; it is closed on Saturdays. For the older places on the same strip, see our Corydon Avenue guide.

The Forks: Colleen and Skyr

Inside The Forks Market at 1 Forks Market Road, Colleen opened in late November 2025. Tristian Schneider and chef Hayden Fellner make the mozzarella and burrata in house and serve fresh pasta and olive-oil soft serve. Skyr, also in the market, opened in early September 2025. It makes Icelandic-style skyr on site and serving smørrebrød and salads, all meat-free. Our guide to The Forks has the rest of the market.

Exchange District: Select Start and SOHO

Select Start opened on January 30, 2025, on the second floor of 110 Market Avenue, as a barcade: classic arcade cabinets, cocktails and snacks such as spicy chicken bao and spam fries. Nearby, SOHO kitchen + wine at Unit A, 100 King Street opened in September 2025 and does European breakfast and lunch from chef Oleksandr Bohdan, with salmon pancakes, Benedicts, arancini and veal ossobuco on the menu. More on the neighbourhood in our Exchange District guide.

Farther out: Isekai Ramen, Dave and LaVerne's, The Forge Bagel Co.

Three 2025 openings are worth a drive. Isekai Ramen, at 1039 Cathedral Avenue in the North End, opened in January 2025; the room has a fantasy-game theme and Phoenix Volcano is the signature ramen. Dave and LaVerne's Modern Diner, at 15 Lakewood Boulevard in Southdale, also opened in January 2025 and does smash burgers and a pastrami Reuben. The Forge Bagel Co., at 692 St. Anne's Road in St. Vital, opened on November 15, 2025, with hand-rolled bagels and its own house coffee.

Before you go: new restaurants change their hours and menus often in their first year, and several on this list close one or two days a week. Check each restaurant's website or social page, and book ahead for Snack Häus, Peacock and Shirley's.
