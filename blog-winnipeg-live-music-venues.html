Where to See Live Music in Winnipeg

Winnipeg Guides • 5 min read • September 13, 2026

Winnipeg's music scene runs from a restored 1907 downtown theatre to dive bars that have booked touring roots acts for decades, plus a warm-weather beer garden that turns into a stage most weeks of the summer. None of it is concentrated in one strip, so a night out chasing live music usually means picking a neighbourhood and a genre first, then the venue.

Burton Cummings Theatre, downtown

The Burton Cummings Theatre at 364 Smith Street opened in 1907 as the Walker Theatre, closed for over a decade in the 1930s and 40s, and went through major renovations in 1990-91 and again in 2009-10. It was renamed in 2002 for the Winnipeg-born Guess Who and solo singer, and was designated a National Historic Site of Canada in 1991. With about 1,600 seats, it's the city's grand room for touring concerts, comedy, and Broadway-style shows, and worth a look for the restored interior alone even before the lights go down.

The Park Theatre, South Osborne

The Park Theatre at 698 Osborne St is a converted neighbourhood cinema that now runs as a mid-size concert hall, small enough that the room still feels intimate but large enough to draw acts that have outgrown a bar stage. It anchors the South Osborne strip, so a show there pairs easily with dinner along the same stretch beforehand.

Times Change(d) High & Lonesome Club, Exchange District

Times Change(d) at 234 Main Street is a long-running dive bar built around blues, folk, and roots music, with a reputation among musicians for booking serious touring and local talent in a room that has never bothered updating its decor. It's the kind of venue where the sound and the crowd matter more than the surroundings.

Good Will Social Club, West End

Good Will Social Club at 625 Portage Ave combines a bar, coffee shop, and all-ages-friendly venue space under one roof in the West End. It leans toward local bands and touring indie and pop-punk acts, with a calendar that runs several nights most weeks.

The Beer Can, a summer-season riverside stage

The Beer Can is a seasonal outdoor beer garden built into shipping containers, with picnic tables strung along the riverbank downtown. Alongside the tap list of Manitoba craft beer, it runs regular live sets and DJ nights through the warmer months; check its own social channels before heading over, since as an open-air venue its calendar and hours shift with the season and the weather.

Check before you go: show schedules, cover charges, and age policies vary by venue and by night. Each of the venues above posts its own calendar on its website or social media, which is more reliable than a general listings site for exact dates and prices.
