Winnipeg Transit basics: getting downtown from Corydon

🚌 Winnipeg Guides • ⏱️ 4 min read • 📅 August 2, 2026

You don't need a car to get from the Corydon/Crescentwood Airbnb into downtown Winnipeg. Between a bus route that runs along Corydon Avenue itself and a rapid transit line a short walk away in Osborne Village, getting downtown is straightforward once you know the basics.

The Corydon route

Corydon Avenue is served directly by its own Winnipeg Transit bus route, so you can catch a bus right on the street the Airbnb sits near rather than walking out to a bigger arterial road first. Because routes and schedules do shift from time to time, check winnipegtransit.com or the Navigo trip planner for the current route number, stop locations, and live arrival times before you head out.

BLUE rapid transit from Osborne Village

For a faster trip downtown, it's worth the walk over to Osborne Village, just east of the Corydon/Crescentwood area. Osborne Village is served by the BLUE rapid transit line, which runs on the dedicated Southwest Transitway and into downtown along Portage Avenue and Main Street — a quicker, more frequent ride than a regular bus route stuck in traffic. BLUE also connects south toward the University of Manitoba if your trip is headed the other way.

Paying your fare

Winnipeg Transit uses the peggo card system: tap a peggo card against the reader on the farebox when you board, or pay cash directly to the driver if you don't have a card. As of 2026, cash fare is $3.45 per ride, with a lower per-ride rate on a loaded peggo card and a monthly unlimited pass available for frequent riders. Fares can change, so confirm current pricing on the Winnipeg Transit fares page before your trip.

Planning your trip

The easiest way to plan a specific trip is the Navigo trip planner on winnipegtransit.com, or a transit app like Google Maps or Transit, both of which pull live Winnipeg Transit schedule data. Enter the Corydon/Crescentwood area as your starting point and your downtown destination to see real-time options and estimated travel time.

Good to know: Winnipeg's transit network has seen route changes in recent years, so don't rely on an old map or an out-of-date app screenshot. Check winnipegtransit.com or the Navigo trip planner the day of your trip for the current route number and next departure from a stop near the Airbnb.
