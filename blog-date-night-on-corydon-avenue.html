A date night on Corydon Avenue near the Airbnb

🍷 Winnipeg Guides • ⏱️ 4 min read • 📅 August 9, 2026

One of the best things about the Corydon/Crescentwood Airbnb is that a proper date night doesn't require a car, a rideshare, or much planning at all. Corydon Avenue is Winnipeg's Little Italy strip, and it's built for exactly this: dinner, a short walk, and a drink to close out the night, all within a few blocks of the front door.

Start with dinner

For a classic Italian dinner, Bar Italia (737 Corydon Ave) and Passero Restaurant (774 Corydon Ave) are both sit-down anchors of the strip, and Colosseo Ristorante Italiano (670 Corydon Ave) is another long-running option if you'd rather compare menus before deciding. If you want something outside the Italian lineup, Saperavi Georgian Cuisine (709 Corydon Ave) is worth the detour — it's the first Georgian restaurant on the Canadian Prairies, open for dinner Tuesday through Sunday (closed Mondays), with a menu built around traditional Georgian dishes.

Book ahead on weekends

Corydon's restaurants are neighbourhood-sized, not built for big walk-in crowds, and the strip gets busy on Friday and Saturday nights, especially with patios open through the summer. A same-day or next-day reservation is usually enough on a weeknight, but for a weekend date night it's worth calling or booking online a few days out.

Finish with a nightcap

For a drink after dinner, The Roost (651 Corydon Ave) is the strip's dedicated cocktail bar, with a rooftop patio and a menu built for sharing if you've still got room. It's an easy walk from any of the dinner spots above, so there's no need to plan transportation between courses.

From the Airbnb: Everything here is on Corydon Avenue itself, within easy walking distance of the front door. See our Corydon patios guide if you'd rather eat outside, or our Osborne Village vs Corydon post if you want a busier, later night instead.
