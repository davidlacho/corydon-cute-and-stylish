Late-night bites near Corydon after 10pm

Winnipeg Guides • 4 min read • July 28, 2026

Corydon Avenue winds down earlier than downtown or Osborne Village, most of Little Italy's kitchens are closed by 9 or 10pm. But a few spots on the strip keep serving well past then, so a late arrival or a post-midnight craving doesn't have to mean a trip off Corydon entirely.

Pizza past midnight

Santa Lucia Pizza (905 Corydon Ave) is the most dependable late option on the avenue: open until midnight Monday to Thursday and Sunday, and until 1am Friday and Saturday, with delivery and takeout through the night on top of dine-in.

A Little Italy bar that keeps going

Bar Italia (737 Corydon Ave) is open until 2am every night of the week, pairing its Italian small plates and cocktails with a lively patio scene and the occasional DJ, the closest thing on Corydon to a proper late-night hangout.

Cocktails and small plates

The Roost (651 Corydon Ave), the second-floor cocktail bar in a converted Little Italy house, serves its seasonal small-plates menu until midnight Sunday through Thursday and until 2am on Fridays and Saturdays, worth a look if you want something quieter than a bar crowd with your late drink.

Coffee and a quick bite

If it's still before 11pm and you just want coffee, a donut, or something simple, Tim Horton's (949 Corydon Ave) stays open until 11pm daily.

From the Airbnb: All four of these are within a few minutes' walk of each other on Corydon itself, so there's no need to leave the strip for a late bite. Bar and kitchen closing times shift with the season and staffing, so it's worth a quick call or a check of the business's own page if you're cutting it close to closing.
