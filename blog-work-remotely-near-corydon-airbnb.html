Where to work remotely near the Corydon Airbnb

Winnipeg Guides • 4 min read • August 10, 2026

If you're staying at the Corydon/Crescentwood Airbnb and need a few hours to get work done, you don't have to camp out at the kitchen table the whole trip. A couple of spots on Corydon Avenue are genuinely set up for laptop work, and there's a quiet public library backup if you'd rather not order anything.

The reliable option: Thom Bargen Coffee Roasters

Thom Bargen Coffee Roasters (743 Corydon Ave) is the neighbourhood's go-to for working with a laptop; it's listed on multiple laptop-friendly-cafe directories for offering free Wi-Fi, plenty of power outlets, and comfortable seating, and it keeps later hours on evenings and weekends if you need an extended session. It's a working coffee shop first, so expect background noise and buy something periodically if you're staying a while.

A longer sit with food nearby: Cafe 22

Cafe 22 (823 Corydon Ave) also offers free Wi-Fi and has a relaxed, lounge-like atmosphere with a long list of hours (open into the evening daily, later on weekends), so it works if you want to combine a work block with lunch or an early dinner rather than just a coffee.

Backup for quiet, no-purchase-required work: Cornish Library

If you'd rather not work from a café table, the City of Winnipeg's Cornish Library (20 West Gate) has free Wi-Fi and bookable computers, and is a short drive from Corydon. Public library hours can shift with the season, so it's worth checking the current hours on the City of Winnipeg's library site before you head over.

Good to know: Wi-Fi availability and seating can change day to day, especially during busy patio season on Corydon. If a laptop session is important to your day, it's worth calling ahead or checking a spot's current hours before you commit to it.
