# Where each post's facts came from

**Internal. Nothing in this file is published.** It exists because source notes
used to be written into the posts themselves, in a guest-facing `.info-box`
headed "Sources and what to re-check". The owner flagged that on 2026-09-27 as a
note to ourselves that had no business being on a page a visitor reads, and the
boxes were removed from all seven posts that carried one. Their text was moved
here verbatim.

Record the provenance of a post's facts here when you publish or refresh it, and
keep the page itself to at most one short, guest-useful line telling the reader
where to confirm before they travel. See "Sources belong in our notes, not on the
page" in `AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md`.

Newest first.

## blog-icelandic-festival-gimli.html (published 2026-10-26)

**The festival and its dates.** The organiser's own release for the 2026
festival gives it as the 137th Íslendingadagurinn under the theme
"Interconnected Shores / Tengdar Strendur", with Icelandic dignitaries
attending, and names the Monday traditional program in Gimli Park where the
Toast to Canada is presented and the Fjallkona brings greetings. Two independent
event calendars give the 2026 run as July 31 to August 3, which matches the
organiser's "every August long weekend". No 2027 dates had been published
anywhere, so the post states the long-weekend pattern and the calendar fact that
the 2027 holiday Monday is August 2, and sends the reader to
icelandicfestival.com. Nothing on the page presents an inferred date as
announced.

**Schedule items.** The 2025 event-schedule PDF on the organiser's site places
the parade on the Monday at 10:00 a.m. from the Gimli Rec Centre with
registration an hour earlier, and Íslendingadunk at Gimli Harbour on the Sunday
afternoon with registration beforehand; a 2024 festival release describes the
dunk as two competitors on a soap-lubricated pole over the water with a sack
holding a wet sponge. A race-registration listing puts the 10 mile, 5K and 1
mile on Sunday, August 2, 2026. All of these are written on the page as "in past
years" or "in recent years", because only the organiser's current schedule can
confirm them for a given year.

**Cost.** The only price the post states is the Odin's Festival Pass, $99 for
the 2026 festival, from the organiser's own shop (shop.icelandicfestival.com),
which describes priority seating at some events, skip-the-line access to the
Vingólf beverage garden with drink tickets, limited quantities and pickup in
Gimli rather than mailing. No source states whether general entry to the
festival grounds is free or ticketed, so the post makes no claim either way and
says the organiser sets what is ticketed each year. An earlier draft called the
dunk "the best free spectacle of the weekend"; "free" was removed for this
reason.

**Getting there.** Manitoba Highway 8 runs from Winnipeg's north limit, where it
meets Route 180 (McPhillips Street), north through Winnipeg Beach and Gimli to
Hecla-Grindstone Provincial Park, posted at 100 km/h. Gimli tourism material
gives the town as about an hour north of Winnipeg on Highway 9; a travel write-up
gives roughly 90 km and about an hour and a quarter in summer conditions; a local
operator says Highway 8 is the most direct and Highways 7 and 9 also work. The
post gives the range rather than a single figure and states no drive time from
the house, which is not in the verified distance table in
`AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md`.

**In town.** The Gimli Art Club's own page dates the seawall mural project to
1997; sources count 72, "more than 70" and "more than 35" murals, so the post
says more than seventy and notes they are repainted. Tourism and heritage
material gives the pier with its small lighthouse, the hill in Harbour Park, and
the fifteen-foot Viking at the south end of Second Avenue, designed by Gissur
Eliasson of the University of Manitoba and built by sculptor George Barone, with
the present Viking Park completed in 2017. Manitoba heritage records and the
Manitoba Historical Society give H. P. Tergesen & Sons at 82 First Avenue,
opened for business on 1 January 1899 and still trading under that name; a
Wikivoyage listing gives the Ship & Plough at 42 Centre St. Both were added to
the ledger. Kris' Fish & Chips, Beach Boy and a bakery named in the same search
results were left off the page: one listing described Kris' as having been
temporarily closed, and nothing current confirmed the others' trading status.

**Daylight.** Sunset at Gimli (50.63 N, 96.99 W) on the 2027 long weekend
computes to about 9:16 p.m. CDT on July 30 and 9:11 p.m. on August 2, so the
page says "around a quarter past nine".

## blog-folklorama-2027.html (published 2026-10-25)

**Dates and scale.** Folklorama's own site gives the 56th festival as August 1
to 14, 2027; a major-events aggregator lists the same Sunday-to-Saturday window
as a 14-day event. The 2026 edition was the 55th, ran August 2 to 15 and listed
45 pavilions including two new ones (festival materials and a CCFM event
listing). The post states the 2027 dates and the 45-pavilion figure only as a
2026 number. Attendance (362,397 in 2026, per the festival's post-event report)
was found but deliberately left off the page; a visitor planning a night does
not need it.

**Two-week split.** Wikipedia and CTV's week-by-week pavilion listings both
describe roughly half the pavilions running in week one and half in week two,
with pavilions rarely open in both. Written on the page as "roughly half", not
as a rule.

**Venue reassignment.** The Centre culturel franco-manitobain, 340 Provencher
Blvd, has hosted the French-Canadian Pavilion (CCFM's own 2025 event page) and,
in an earlier edition, the Ghana Pavilion (a CTV pavilion listing). That pair is
the source for the warning that a hall's pavilion changes between years. No
claim is made about which pavilion is there in any particular year.

**Admission.** Folklorama pavilion pages list General Admission at $7.50 per
ticket plus applicable service fee, non-refundable, and the admission-options
page states children 12 and under are free with a paying adult to a maximum of
five children per adult, with the free ticket still requiring a booking. Online
sales close one hour before each showtime unless sold out. Tickets guarantee
admission only and seats are not reserved, except where a pavilion takes dinner
reservations or where a VIP tour includes seating; the festival's own advice is
to arrive early for the best seats. Outside food and drink are prohibited.
Payment methods vary by pavilion and are listed in the festival's travel guide.
The $7.50 is labelled on the page as a 2026 figure.

**Showtimes and closing times.** 6:45, 8:15 and 9:45 p.m. is the common pattern
across individual 2026 pavilion pages (Ukraine Kyiv, British Isles, Greek,
Japanese), with earlier weekend slots at some. The Greek Pavilion's page gives
doors 30 to 45 minutes before a show and a show length of about 45 minutes. The
Scandinavian Pavilion listed 6:00 to 9:30 on Sunday and Monday and 6:00 to 11:00
Tuesday to Saturday; Ukraine Kyiv listed closings to 11 on weeknights and to
midnight on Friday and Saturday. A third-party planning site claiming a
festival-wide 5 to 10 p.m. was not used, since it contradicts the pavilions'
own pages. The post therefore says the times vary and names the common pattern.

**Getting between pavilions.** There is no festival-wide shuttle. Folklorama
runs its own VIP bus tours (two or three pavilions, coach transport, admission,
reserved seating and a meal spread across the stops), custom group tours, and
added pedal-powered tours between selected pavilions in 2026. The bus operator
for the tours is Beaver Bus Lines; the firms are not named on the page, so no
ledger entries were needed.

**Transit.** The City of Winnipeg's fares table gives full fare $3.45 cash
(exact change), $3.10 peggo e-cash, and a 24-hour e-pass at $11.45, with a cash
transfer good for 90 minutes in any direction. The break-even arithmetic on the
page (the day pass wins on the fourth ride) follows from those figures.

**Distances.** Only the verified table in `AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md` was used:
Centre culturel franco-manitobain 11 min / 5.1 km, Portage and Main 9 min /
3.9 km, The Forks 10 min / 3.9 km, Corydon Avenue about ten minutes on foot
south. No drive time is given for any pavilion venue not in that table.

**Daylight.** Early-August sunset in Winnipeg falls around nine in the evening,
which is the basis for "the sun is still up when the 6:45 show begins and well
down by the time the last one lets out". Astronomical, no source needed beyond
the latitude.

**Not stated anywhere on the page:** the 2027 pavilion list, any pavilion's
2027 venue, week or showtime, any 2027 price, and any attendance figure.
folklorama.ca does not resolve from this sandbox (DNS ENOTFOUND through the
agent proxy), so everything above came through search result summaries.

## blog-winnipeg-folk-festival.html (refreshed 2026-10-21)

Checked 2026-10-21 for the 2027 edition. The sandbox could not resolve
`winnipegfolkfestival.ca` directly, so every festival fact below comes from
search results quoting the festival's own pages (FAQ, Tickets, Transportation,
How to Folk / What to Know Before You Go, Family Area, Quiet Campground) plus
Winnipeg Transit's own announcement of the shuttle.

- **Dates and edition:** the festival FAQ's own future-dates list gives
  `2027 - July 8-11`, and the homepage bills it as the 52nd Edition, July 8 to
  11, 2027. The FAQ also states the festival always takes the second weekend in
  July, which is why the queue's "typical window" guess of July 8 to 11 turned
  out to be the confirmed date.
- **Venue:** Birds Hill Provincial Park.
- **Tickets:** sold online through Front Gate Tickets
  (`winnipegfolkfestival.frontgatetickets.com`) or in person at the festival
  office, 203-211 Bannatyne Avenue. Not sold at the Main Gate. Limit of four
  tickets per person and one RV/trailer permit; all sales final except a full
  event cancellation. The 2026 cycle put RV/trailer permits on sale
  2025-12-08 at 10am, which is why the page says on-sale dates land "usually in
  December"; **no 2027 on-sale date or price is stated on the page.**
- **Children and youth:** 12 and under free when accompanied by an adult and no
  camping ticket required; 13 to 17 need a youth camping ticket (festival FAQ
  and the 2026 Front Gate listing).
- **Folk Fest Express:** free Winnipeg Transit shuttle, downtown stop on
  southbound Memorial Boulevard between St. Mary and York, ending on Festival
  Drive in the park; all buses accessible; return stop on Festival Drive across
  from the west parking lot, reached by the crosswalk; last bus departs after
  the Main Stage set ends, with the festival's own warning to check Transit
  schedules for a connecting route. (Festival Transportation page; Winnipeg
  Transit post.) Note: a Roadtrip Manitoba guide gives the downtown stop as
  *northbound* Memorial between St. Mary and Portage. The festival's own wording
  was used.
- **Campground Shuttle:** every 30 minutes between the Quiet Campground, the
  Birds Hill Provincial Park Campground and the festival site; not accessible.
- **Parking:** arrivals from the west directed to Festival West Parking; no
  overnight parking in festival lots.
- **Tarps:** maximum 8 by 10 feet; the festival asks that markers not obstruct
  the view behind; tarp tickets handed out about 10 minutes before the gate
  opens in a line along the tree line at the Main Gate; shuffle begins on the
  Main Stage field about 20 minutes after the gate opens, or 5:30 if the ground
  is wet and the field is roped off; Big Bluestem is a dancing zone at night
  with no tarps. **The daily gate time itself was not found in any source, so
  the page never states one** and describes the shuffle only relative to the
  gate opening.
- **Camping:** Quiet Campground about 4 km from the festival site with a free
  shuttle, opening at noon on the Wednesday; Birds Hill Provincial Park's own
  gates closed 11pm to 7am daily. Festival Camping has sold out in past years
  while Quiet Camping remained available (Manitoba Music news item).
- **Stages:** Chickadee Big Top, the stilt walkers and the instrument petting
  zoo come from the festival's own Family Area page. The day stage names
  (Snowberry Field, Bur Oak, Green Ash, Shady Grove, Spruce Hollow, Little Stage
  in the Forest, Big Bluestem, Northern Lights Main Stage) come from a 2025
  third-party listing, so the page presents them as names the stages "have
  carried for years" rather than as a confirmed 2027 stage list.
- **Daylight:** Winnipeg sunset in the second week of July is about 9:30pm
  (astronomical, not sourced to the festival).
- **Distance from the house:** only RBC Convention Centre, 7 min / 3.0 km, from
  the verified table in `AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md`. No house-to-Birds-Hill drive
  time is stated anywhere on the page, and the page says so explicitly rather
  than estimating.
- **No lineup is named for 2027.** None had been announced in anything found.
- **Editing-pass note:** the first draft of the "Where to stay" section said on
  the page that we would not quote a drive time we had not measured. That is our
  own process facing the reader, which the "Sources belong in our notes" rule
  forbids, so it was cut before the push and replaced with a plain reason to take
  the shuttle. The underlying rule still held: no house-to-Birds-Hill time is
  stated.
