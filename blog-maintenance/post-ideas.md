# Daily post idea queue

The cron publishes one fresh, guest-relevant post per run. Pick the FIRST unused
idea from the queue below (or invent a comparable one if the queue is thin),
build the post, then move it into "Used" with its date and slug so topics never
repeat. Write a **general Winnipeg guide** that a visitor anywhere in the city
would search for: neighbourhood guides across the whole city, seasonal things to
do, food and drink, arts and sport, transit, day trips, practical Manitoba tips.
Match the tone and structure of existing `blog-*.html` posts. Never invent facts
(hours, prices, event dates) that you cannot verify; keep unverifiable specifics
general.

**Two standing rules — read these before picking a topic:**

1. **Never a pet-friendly post.** The home does not accept pets. No dogs, dog
   parks, off-leash areas, leash bylaws, pet-friendly patios, or travelling with
   an animal — not as a post, a section, or a listicle entry. Never add such an
   idea to this queue. See the hard content rule in `AGENT-INSTRUCTIONS.md`.
2. **City-wide by default.** At most one post in four may be anchored to
   Corydon/Crescentwood or the Airbnb's immediate surroundings, and slugs must
   not default to `-near-corydon-airbnb`. Check the last four "Used" entries
   before choosing. The listing gets one short closing paragraph at most.
