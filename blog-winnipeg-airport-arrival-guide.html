Landing at YWG: getting from the airport into Winnipeg

Winnipeg Guides • 5 min read • August 27, 2026

Winnipeg James Armstrong Richardson International Airport, known by its code YWG, is the city's only major commercial airport and sits a short distance from downtown, generally a 15 to 25 minute drive depending on traffic. The terminal is a single, compact building, easy to find your way through even on a first visit, with taxis, rideshare, transit, and rental car counters all reachable on foot from arrivals.

Taxi and rideshare

A staffed taxi stand sits just outside arrivals, with local operators including Unicity Taxi. Uber operates at the airport with a designated pickup point; rideshare coverage can shift, so it's worth checking which apps are currently active before you land rather than assuming. Fares vary with traffic and demand, so check the app or ask the taxi dispatcher for an estimate before you get in rather than assuming a fixed rate.

Winnipeg Transit from the airport

Winnipeg Transit runs bus service to and from the airport, with the stop on the ground transportation level outside arrivals. It is the cheapest way into the city, though a transit trip downtown generally takes longer than a taxi, in the range of 30 to 40 minutes with a transfer depending on your destination. Winnipeg Transit's route network has been reorganized in recent years, so check the current airport service page or the Transit app for the exact route and schedule on the day you travel rather than relying on an old route number.

Renting a car

Four rental car counters sit in the parkade directly across from the terminal, with the major agencies, including Avis, Budget, Enterprise, Hertz, and National/Alamo, represented on site. It's a short walk from baggage claim, so renting on arrival doesn't add much time to the trip.

Before you go

Because YWG is a smaller airport than what many visitors are used to, lines tend to move quickly and the whole terminal is walkable in a few minutes. That said, staff, counter hours, and route numbers can all change, so it's worth a quick check of the airport's own site or the Transit app close to your travel date rather than planning entirely from an older guide.

Staying at the Corydon Airbnb: the Corydon/Crescentwood area is a roughly 15 to 20 minute drive from the airport depending on traffic, so a taxi or rideshare straight from arrivals is the simplest way to reach the door.
