Winterruption WPG 2027, January 20 to 24

Winnipeg Guides • 5 min read • November 22, 2026

Winterruption WPG runs for five nights, Wednesday January 20 to Sunday January 24, 2027. There is no festival site. Real Love Winnipeg and the West End Cultural Centre lay a schedule over seven existing rooms, among them two theatres, a bowling alley and a handful of bars, and the whole thing happens indoors in the coldest week of a Winnipeg January. That structure is the part worth understanding before you buy anything, because it decides what a ticket gets you and how far you will be walking between sets.

Two ways in. The festival sells an all-access wristband, described on its own ticket page as one wristband, every show, all five days, and it sells each show separately through Showpass. Sales opened on October 12. The festival prints one exception next to the wristband: Winnipeg Pro Wrestling, at the West End Cultural Centre on the Friday, is not included. Single-show prices sit on Showpass rather than on the festival's page, and the only figure printed beside a show on that page is for Reuben and the Dark at the Park Theatre on Friday January 22, from $30 plus fees. Take any other number you find elsewhere with suspicion and check Showpass.

Three of the seven rooms are on one block of Osborne

The Park Theatre sits at 698 Osborne Street, Sidestage at 700 and Park Alleys at 730. You can stand outside one and see the next. On the Thursday, the Friday and the Saturday all three run at once with staggered start times, which is the festival's actual design: Thursday night puts Janky Bungag on at the Park Theatre at 8 p.m. while Gizmo plays Sidestage at 8:15, and you are meant to move. The other four rooms are spread out. The West End Cultural Centre is at 586 Ellice Avenue, the Handsome Daughter at 61 Sherbrook Street, Public Domain at 633 Portage Avenue, and Cornerstone, which hosts Chad VanGaalen on the opening Wednesday, at 300 Hugo Street North. Those four are separate trips, not a stroll, so pick a room for the night rather than planning to cross town mid-evening in January.

The headline bookings are spaced out deliberately. Grouper plays the West End Cultural Centre on Thursday at 8 p.m. and Bill Callahan closes the same room on Sunday at 8 p.m., with Habibi finishing Sidestage that night at 9:30. DJ Shub headlines Public Domain late on the Friday. Cloud Rat and Blue Hawaii land on the Saturday, in different rooms, an hour apart.

One room is 18+, and two nights run on a split schedule

Everything on the 2027 schedule is listed all ages except Public Domain, which is 18+ on both the Friday and the Saturday. If you are bringing someone under eighteen, that is the one room to plan around. The Handsome Daughter splits the Friday and the Saturday into an early show and a late show with separate doors, 6 p.m. and 9 p.m. on the Saturday, which means two tickets' worth of music in one building and a gap in the middle long enough to go and eat.

Doors run from about 6:15 p.m. The sun sets at 6:03 p.m. on January 20 and 6:06 p.m. on the 22nd, so every set of the festival is played after dark, and the day is under nine hours long. Coat check where a room has one. A parka is a burden for five hours in a small bar, and the walk between the Osborne rooms is short enough that you will not want to put it back on.

Before you go: Lineups, set times and room assignments shift as the festival gets closer, and the schedule page says so. Confirm the night you want at winterruptionwpg.ca in the week before you travel.

If late January is the week you are coming, the Winnipeg New Music Festival usually falls in the same stretch, which makes for an odd and rather good pairing of a composer's festival and a basement one. Where to stay for Winterruption

Our three-bedroom house in Crescentwood sits between the two halves of the festival map. Cornerstone, on Hugo Street North, is in our own pocket of the city and close enough to walk to; the Osborne rooms, the West End Cultural Centre and Public Domain are each a short drive, on the same side of the Assiniboine or just across it.

A five-night festival in the third week of January is not a weekend trip, and the rooms it uses are small, which tends to mean people come for two or three nights rather than one. Late January is also when the river trail skating season is usually running. If Winterruption is the reason for the trip, book the room before you buy the wristband.
