Gene Simmons Band at the Centennial Concert Hall, December 9, 2026

Winnipeg Guides • 5 min read • November 19, 2026

The Gene Simmons Band plays the Centennial Concert Hall, 555 Main Street, on Wednesday, December 9, 2026, at 8 p.m. Tickets go through the hall: centennialconcerthall.com, the box office in person, or 204-949-3999. They went on sale back on May 1, so the question now is what is left rather than when to buy.

Two things about that listing confuse people searching for it, and both are worth clearing up before you book. The first is the name of the room. Resale sites file this show under Manitoba Centennial Concert Hall; the hall and Tourism Winnipeg both call it the Centennial Concert Hall. Same building, same 555 Main Street address, one date. The second is the billing. This is the Gene Simmons Band, Simmons touring with his own group, not KISS, and the Canadian run puts Winnipeg between Saskatoon on December 7 and Sydney, Nova Scotia, on December 12. The booking itself tells you the scale: a seated theatre on Main Street rather than the downtown arena.

What the 8 p.m. on the ticket actually means

No support act appears on any listing for this date, which makes 8 p.m. the time the lights go down rather than the time somebody you did not come for walks out. Plan backwards from it, because December in Winnipeg does not leave you much margin. The sun is gone before half past four in early December, so by eight o'clock the cold has had three and a half hours of darkness to settle in, and the walk from wherever you leave the car is the part of the evening that catches people out.

Parking is the indoor parkade behind the hall, entered from Lily Avenue. Clearance is six feet ten inches, which rules out a tall van or a roof box, and nobody discovers this until they are already nose-up at the ramp. Accessible parking is arranged through Imperial Parking on 204-943-3578. There is a bike rack inside the parkade, free for event patrons, which in December is a theoretical pleasure. Walk in from the Main Street frontage: the Manitoba Centennial Centre entrance off Rupert Avenue is still out of use while museum construction goes on, and people keep walking round to it anyway.

By bus you are on the Main Street corridor, routes FX2, FX3, F8, D16 and 43, with stops 10629 and 10635 at the door. That saves you the parkade exit queue at the end of a rock show, when everyone wants the same ramp at once. It costs you a wait at a stop late on a December night, which is a real trade and not everyone's to make. Our transit basics page covers fares and transfers if you have not used Winnipeg Transit before.

Inside, coat check is in the main lobby behind the feature-wall mural. Use it. A parka folded under a Concert Hall seat is a nuisance for you and for whoever is beside you. Glass containers and outside food do not come into the theatre.

Prices, and why this page does not quote one

Aggregators and resale sites carry numbers for this show that disagree with each other by a wide margin, and at least some of them are quoted in US dollars without saying so. A face-value seat and a resale seat are not the same purchase. Take the figure from the hall's own page or the box office, and if what you find is a resale listing, check what the fees do to it before you decide the price is the price.

Before you go: Start times, seat availability and the parkade rate can all change between now and December 9. Confirm the performance at centennialconcerthall.com or on 204-949-3999, and check the parking rate in the week you travel.

If the concert is the anchor of a December trip rather than all of it, the hall is busy either side of this date: Jann Arden plays the same stage on December 8, the Winnipeg Symphony's Christmas run takes the same stage on the 3rd, the 5th, the 6th, the 12th and the 13th, and Christmas in Winnipeg covers the markets and the lights around them.

Where to stay for the December 9 show

Our three-bedroom house in Crescentwood is ten minutes from the hall by car, 4.3 kilometres, over the Assiniboine and then north. For a show that ends late on a work night, the distance between a seat and a warm room is most of what you are buying. Early and mid December fill quickly here: this same week holds the symphony's Christmas programs and Jets home games on the 8th and the 10th, and a Wednesday concert draws people who need the Tuesday and Wednesday nights rather than a weekend.
