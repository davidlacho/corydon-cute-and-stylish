WSO From the Inside Out, January 14 to 16, 2027

Winnipeg Guides • 5 min read • November 21, 2026

The Winnipeg Symphony Orchestra plays From the Inside Out on Thursday January 14, Friday January 15 and Saturday January 16, 2027, each night at 7:30 p.m., at the Centennial Concert Hall, 555 Main Street. The stage is rebuilt for it and the audience sits in the middle of the orchestra. Below are the questions people ask us about this one, answered in the order they tend to come up.

What does sitting "inside" the orchestra actually mean?

The WSO reconfigures the stage and seats part of the audience among the players rather than out in the house, so the sound arrives from all sides instead of from in front of you. Nothing is amplified. There are no speakers and no headphones, which is the point: the second violins are a few feet to your left and you hear the room doing its work on them. If you have only ever heard an orchestra from row M, this is a different physical experience of the same music.

What is the orchestra playing, and who is conducting?

Naomi Woo conducts. She was an assistant conductor here before moving on to work with the Philadelphia Orchestra, so this is a return rather than a debut. The programme is four short works: the overture to Ethel Smyth's The Wreckers, Bizet's first Carmen suite, Isabella Gellis's Four Morris Interludes, and Grieg's first Peer Gynt suite. Nothing on that list runs long, and three of the four are music you will recognise within a bar or two even if you cannot name it.

How do you get tickets?

Through the orchestra, not the hall. The Centennial Concert Hall's own season page points buyers to the WSO for this series, which means wso.ca or 204-949-3999. The box office sells Wednesday to Saturday from 11 a.m. to 4 p.m., and it also opens in the Concert Hall lobby an hour before each performance for pickup and last-minute seats. The WSO has not published a price for this concert, and the figures that float around on resale and listing sites are not the orchestra's own, so ask the box office what a seat among the players costs rather than trusting a number you found elsewhere.

Which of the three nights should you take?

All three start at 7:30 p.m. with the same programme, so pick on what else is happening downtown. Saturday the 16th is the busiest of the three: the Jets host Anaheim at Canada Life Centre the same night, and the downtown parkades and the Portage Avenue approaches feel it. Thursday the 14th is the quietest drive in and the easiest night to find a table beforehand. Friday sits in between.

What is the walk from the car like in mid-January?

Dark and short, if you plan it. The sun sets at about 4:55 p.m. that week, giving Winnipeg roughly eight and a half hours of daylight, so by 7:30 the cold has had hours to settle. The indoor parkade behind the hall is entered from Lily Avenue and has a clearance of six feet ten inches, which rules out a tall van or a roof box; accessible parking is arranged through Imperial Parking on 204-943-3578. By bus you are on the Main Street corridor, routes FX2, FX3, F8, D16 and 43, with stops 10629 and 10635 at the hall's door. Our transit basics page covers fares if you have not used Winnipeg Transit before. Whichever way you arrive, use the lobby coat check. A parka under a Concert Hall seat is a nuisance for you and for the person beside you, and on this night you may be sitting closer to strangers than usual.

Is this a sensible first symphony concert?

Yes, and the orchestra pitches it that way. Short pieces, no interval-length single symphony to sit through, familiar melodies, and a seat that makes the mechanics visible: you watch a horn player count bars and a section leader cue an entry. Children who can sit still for an hour do well with it. Children who cannot will do better at one of the WSO's family concerts.

Where do you eat before a 7:30 start?

North of Portage, within a ten-minute walk. The Exchange District begins a block from the hall and holds most of the options, and Hargrave St. Market is the fallback when the group cannot agree, since everyone orders separately and the kitchens run fast. Book for 5:30 p.m. if you want to be in your seat without rushing.

Before you go: Start times, seat availability and parkade rates can change between now and January. Confirm the performance and the ticket price at wso.ca or on 204-949-3999 in the week before you travel.

If you are building a longer trip around classical music, the WSO's New Music Festival usually follows in the last week of January at the same hall, and our performing arts guide sets the symphony against the ballet, Royal MTC and Manitoba Opera on cost and on what suits children.

Where to stay for From the Inside Out

Our three-bedroom house in Crescentwood is ten minutes from the Concert Hall by car, 4.3 kilometres, north across the Assiniboine. On a January night the distance between the last note and a warm room is most of what a visitor is buying, and a 9 p.m. finish in that weather makes a short drive worth more than it looks on a map. Mid-January weekends here go early: the river trail skating season usually opens in January, and the Jets are home on the 16th, the 19th and the 21st.

If one of these three nights is the reason for the trip, book the room before the ticket. Our Airbnb listing shows what is open that week.
