What's open on Corydon Avenue on a Sunday

Winnipeg Guides • 3 min read • August 11, 2026

Sundays on Corydon Avenue aren't as sleepy as they used to be, but hours still shift enough from weekday hours that it's worth a quick check before you head out from the Airbnb. Here's what's reliably open, one popular spot that's worth skipping that day, and a backup that's open late if your day runs long.

Coffee: Thom Bargen Coffee Roasters

Thom Bargen Coffee Roasters (743 Corydon Ave) keeps Sunday hours of roughly 8am to 9pm, so it's a solid, unhurried start to the day and still an option well into the evening if you want a coffee after dinner.

Early and late backup: Tim Horton's

If you're up before the cafés open, Tim Horton's (949 Corydon Ave) runs Sunday hours of roughly 6am to 11pm, making it the earliest and latest reliable option on this stretch of Corydon.

Lunch or dinner: Tommy's Pizzeria

Tommy's Pizzeria (842 Corydon Ave) is open Sundays too, generally from around noon into the evening. Listed closing times vary a little by source, so if you're planning a later dinner it's worth a quick call to (204) 219-7499 to confirm before you walk over.

One to skip on Sundays: Wako Sushi Café

Worth knowing before you make plans: Wako Sushi Café (875 Corydon Ave) is closed on Sundays, running Monday to Saturday instead. If sushi is the plan, go on a different day of your stay.

Good to know: Independent restaurant hours on Corydon can shift with the season and with staffing, even for spots that are normally open Sundays. If a specific stop is the whole reason for your trip out, a quick call ahead is worth it.
